01 / About

A forum built for voices that matter.

Global Youth Forum creates opportunities for young people to develop leadership, diplomacy, public speaking and collaboration through engaging Model United Nations experiences.

01

Diplomacy

Practice negotiation, public speaking and consensus-building through realistic committee experiences.

02

03

Leadership

Build confidence, research skills and the ability to represent a position with clarity and respect.
